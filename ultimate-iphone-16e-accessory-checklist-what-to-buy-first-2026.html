The Ultimate iPhone 16e Accessory Checklist: What to Buy First (2026 Guide)

Published on January 08, 2026

The iPhone 16e is here, offering the power of the A18 chip in a familiar, compact 6.1-inch design. But as any seasoned iPhone owner knows, the phone is only the beginning. With the move to USB-C and the absence of a wall charger in the box, having the right accessories from day one is the difference between a seamless upgrade and a frustrating afternoon.

Use this checklist to build your perfect iPhone 16e setup.

Start Here Must-Have iPhone 16e Accessories

These are the non-negotiables. If you buy nothing else, ensure these three items are in your cart to keep your phone running and protected.

Protective Case

The iPhone 16e shares its dimensions with the iPhone 13 and 14, but don't be fooled—the single 48MP Fusion camera and the new Action Button require a specific fit. A dedicated case ensures all buttons remain tactile and the lens stays scratch-free.

  • Essential for: Drop protection and style.
  • Shop dedicated iPhone 16e cases

Screen Protector

While the Ceramic Shield is tough, it’s not scratch-proof. A tempered glass protector is a small investment that prevents permanent micro-abrasions from keys, coins, and grit.

  • Essential for: Preserving trade-in value and preventing cracks.
  • View iPhone 16e screen protectors

20W+ USB-C Wall Charger

The iPhone 16e supports fast charging (up to 50% in 30 minutes), but it requires a USB-C Power Delivery (PD) adapter of at least 20W. If you’re coming from a Lightning-era iPhone, your old "cube" won't work with the included cable.

  • Essential for: Actually being able to charge your phone at modern speeds.
  • Shop fast-charging wall adapters

Recommended Accessories for Everyday Use

Once the basics are covered, these items enhance your daily experience and ensure you're never caught with a dead battery.

  • Car Mounts & Car Chargers: The iPhone 16e is your primary navigator. A secure mount and a USB-C car plug are vital for legal, hands-free driving.
  • 2-Metre Charging Cable: The cable in the box is only 1 metre long. A 2-metre braided cable gives you the freedom to use your phone comfortably while it’s plugged in.
  • Bluetooth Audio: With no headphone jack, a pair of wireless earbuds is a must for music and calls.

Build Your iPhone 16e Setup by Budget

Not everyone needs the most expensive gear. Here is how to prioritize:

  • The Essentials ($50–$80): Focus on a high-quality TPU case, a tempered glass screen protector, and a single 20W fast charger.
  • The Pro Setup ($100–$200): Add a MagSafe-compatible case (even though the 16e doesn't have built-in magnets, these cases add the functionality!), a car mount, and a high-capacity power bank.

Based on our 2026 data, these are the items Australian users are grabbing first:

  1. Clear Hybrid Cases: Let the iPhone 16e colours shine while offering military-grade corner protection.
  2. Privacy Screen Protectors: Keep your screen contents hidden from prying eyes on public transport.
  3. GaN 30W Mini Chargers: Faster than the 20W standard and small enough to fit in a coin pocket.

Avoid These Common iPhone 16e Accessory Mistakes

  • Using Old Cables: Don't try to use a "Lightning to USB-C" adapter for charging; they are often unreliable. Stick to pure USB-C to USB-C cables.
  • Skipping the Screen Protector: "I'll get one later" is the most common reason for a $200+ screen repair in the first week.
  • Cheap "No-Name" Chargers: Underpowered or uncertified chargers can overheat your battery and shorten its lifespan.
