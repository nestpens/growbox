Upgrade Your Drive: A Guide to Aerpro Parking Assist Kits

Published on January 11, 2026

We have all been there. You are circling a busy parking lot, you finally spot a space, but it looks just a little too tight. Or perhaps you are backing out of a long driveway, craning your neck, hoping there isn't a low bollard or a stray bicycle in your blind spot. Parking anxiety is a real phenomenon, but it doesn't have to be a permanent part of your driving experience.

Modern vehicle technology has made great strides in safety and parking sensors are among the most valuable upgrades you can add to a car, truck or commercial vehicle. If your vehicle didn't come with these features from the factory, you aren't out of luck. Aftermarket solutions offer professional-grade protection without the need to buy a brand-new car.

Campad Electronics offers a robust selection of Aerpro commercial parking sensor kits designed to suit every type of driver and vehicle. Whether you drive a compact hatchback, a heavy-duty commercial truck or tow a caravan, there is an Aerpro solution ready to help you navigate tight spots with ease.

In this article, we will break down four specific models available at Campad Electronics, comparing their features, pricing and ideal use cases so you can make an informed decision.

Why Invest in Aftermarket Parking Sensors?

Before we dive into the specific models, it is worth understanding why this upgrade is so essential. While backup cameras provide a visual feed, they often distort distance or get obscured by rain and dirt. Acoustic parking sensors add a critical layer of redundancy. They use ultrasonic waves to detect solid objects, providing an audible warning that intensifies as you get closer to an impact.

For commercial drivers, these systems are even more critical. Large vans and trucks have massive blind spots directly behind the vehicle. A reliable sensor system protects the fleet vehicle from damage, reduces insurance liability and, most importantly, protects pedestrians and property.

Campad Electronics stocks Aerpro kits because they are renowned for reliability and ease of installation. Let’s look at the specific options.

The Reliable Standard: Aerpro Wired Rear Parking Sensor Kit (APPS4R)

For many daily drivers, the goal is simple: stop backing into things. If you have a standard passenger vehicle, sedan, hatch or SUV and you want a straightforward, reliable safety upgrade, the Aerpro APPS4R parking sensor kit is your starting point.

Key Features and Functionality

This kit is a wired system, meaning the sensors in your rear bumper are physically connected to the control unit and the beeper. Wired connections are the gold standard for interference-free performance. You don't have to worry about radio frequencies or signal dropouts.

The system utilizes four sensors that mount into your rear bumper. When you engage reverse gear, the system activates automatically. As you approach an obstacle, the separate buzzer provides an audible alert. The beeping speeds up as the distance closes, becoming a solid tone when you are dangerously close.

Ideal Use Case

This model is perfect for the family car or a daily commuter. At a price point of roughly $228.99, it is an affordable insurance policy against cracked bumpers and scratched paint. Because it is wired, installation is best suited for standard-length vehicles where running cables from the bumper to the dashboard or trunk area is manageable.

Why choose this one?

  • Cost-effective: Great entry point for premium safety.
  • Reliability: Wired connection ensures consistent performance.
  • Simplicity: Audible-only alerts keep your eyes on your mirrors, not a screen.

For the Heavy Haulers: Aerpro Wireless Commercial Rear Sensor Kit (APPS4WCR)

Commercial vehicles and trailers present a unique challenge. Running a physical wire from the back of a 20-foot trailer or a long-wheelbase delivery van all the way to the driver's cabin is labour-intensive and sometimes technically impossible. This is where the Aerpro APPS4WCR parking sensor kit shines.

Key Features and Functionality

Priced around $338.99, this kit is designed specifically for the rigors of commercial use. The standout feature here is the "Wireless" designation. The control box at the rear communicates wirelessly with the buzzer unit in the cabin. This eliminates the need to dismantle the entire length of the vehicle interior to run cables.

The sensors are robust and designed to withstand the wear and tear typical of work vehicles. Like the wired version, it uses a progressive audible beeper to warn the driver of obstacles.

Ideal Use Case

This kit is essential for anyone operating a van, truck, ute with a tray or towing a trailer. If you are hitching a caravan or driving a delivery van with no rear-view mirror visibility, this kit acts as your eyes. The wireless range is designed to accommodate longer vehicle lengths that standard wired kits cannot reach.

Why choose this one?

  • Installation Ease: No need to run wires the full length of a truck or trailer.
  • Heavy Duty: Designed for commercial environments.
  • Versatility: Excellent for vehicles that tow trailers.

Total Awareness: Aerpro Wireless Front and Rear Kit With LCD (APPS4WFR)

For those who want complete perimeter protection and prefer visual feedback alongside audio cues, the Aerpro APPS4WFR parking sensor kit is the premium choice. Priced at approximately $648.99, this is a comprehensive system that protects both ends of your vehicle.

Key Features and Functionality

This kit includes eight sensors in total, four for the front bumper and four for the rear. It upgrades the user interface from a simple beeper to a sophisticated LCD display. This display mounts on your dashboard and shows you exactly which sensor is detecting an object and how far away it is.

The wireless functionality here simplifies the installation of the display unit, reducing dashboard clutter. When you put the car in reverse, the rear sensors activate. When you press the brake pedal, the front sensors activate, helping you inch forward into tight garage spaces without hitting the wall.

Ideal Use Case

This is the ultimate kit for newer, larger SUVs or luxury sedans where you want to maintain a pristine condition. If you frequently park in tight city garages or parallel park in busy streets, having both front and rear coverage is a game-changer. The visual LCD aid is particularly helpful for drivers who prefer seeing the distance rather than just hearing it.

Why choose this one?

  • 360-Degree Protection: Covers both front and rear bumpers.
  • Visual Data: LCD screen provides precise distance information.
  • Convenience: Wireless display technology reduces dashboard wiring.

Protecting the Nose: Aerpro Wireless Front Parking Sensor Kit (APPS4WF)

Sometimes, you already have a backup camera or rear sensors, but the front of your car remains vulnerable. High curbs, parking blocks and low walls are the natural enemies of front bumpers and splitters. The Aerpro APPS4WF parking sensor kit is the targeted solution for this problem.

Key Features and Functionality

Available for around $438.99, this kit focuses exclusively on the front of the vehicle. It includes four sensors and an LCD display. Like the full front/rear kit, this system uses a display to show you exactly where the obstacle is relative to your front bumper.

It is particularly useful because judging the distance over a long hood (bonnet) is difficult for even the most experienced drivers. The wireless design ensures that installing the display inside the cabin is straightforward, communicating seamlessly with the front sensor control unit.

Ideal Use Case

This kit is a favorite among sports car owners with low front spoilers that are easily damaged by concrete parking stops. It is also excellent for large trucks or SUVs with high hoods that create a large blind spot immediately in front of the grille.

Why choose this one?

  • Targeted Protection: Perfect if you only need front coverage.
  • Damage Prevention: Saves low bumpers from high curbs.
  • Visual Aid: Includes the LCD display for precision parking.
